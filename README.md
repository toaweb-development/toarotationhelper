# TOA Rotation Helper 0.6.0 — WoW Forever

A Hekili-style rotation helper for WoW: Forever.

```
[ NEXT SPELL ][then][then]   keybind in the corner, gold frame in combat, "R2" = low rank, "AUTO" = wand shooting
[cd][cd][cd]                 the rotation's long cooldowns
```

## Why an own engine

Blizzard's Assisted Combat is switched off in Forever: `C_AssistedCombat.IsAvailable()` returns
`false, "WRONG_WORLD_STATE_EXPRESSION"` and the client's AssistedCombat tables are empty. Forever also
hides cooldowns, mana and the target's debuffs in combat (secret values). What it does not hide is the
player's **own casts**, so the addon follows them:

| The rotation needs | The addon knows it from |
|---|---|
| Is Mind Blast ready? | your last Mind Blast + its cooldown (synced with the real value out of combat) |
| Is SW:P still on the target? | your last SW:P on that target + its duration (+ Improved SW:P) |
| Mana | the real value out of combat, minus the cost of each cast in combat (no regen: errs low) |
| What comes after the current cast | the cast in progress is treated as done, like Hekili |

Spell ranks, costs, cast times, cooldowns and durations come from Forever's own game data
(`Data/Priest.lua`, generated by `tools/generate_data.py`).

## Classes

| Class | Rotations | Status |
|---|---|---|
| Priest | Smite (Discipline/Holy), Shadow, Wand (levelling) | tested on a dummy; levelling rules new in 0.4 |
| Others | Blizzard Assisted Combat if it is ever enabled; otherwise "No rotation for … yet" | planned |

Auto mode picks Shadow once you know Mind Flay; below level 25 with a wand equipped, Wand; otherwise Smite.
`/toarot mode auto|smite|shadow|wand`.

## Rotation research (Priest)

Tested with the [MythicSim Forever engine](https://github.com/sage3648/mythicsim-forever-engine)
(the simulator behind MythicSim's Forever sims), level 60 reference builds, 2000 fights per cell. Only
conditions the addon can follow were kept; fight length and debuff stacks cannot be read in combat.

| Smite, DPS | 30 s | 60 s | 120 s | 300 s |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| MythicSim preset | 564 | 562 | 557 | 533 |
| This addon's rules | **572** | **572** | **559** | **534** |

| Shadow, DPS | 60 s | 120 s | 300 s |
|---|---|---|---|
| MythicSim preset | 334 | 322 | 276 |
| This addon's rules | **337** | **324** | **286** |

Smite: Inner Focus → Holy Fire when Holy Fire is due · SW:P if not up · Devouring Plague · Holy Fire when
under 3 s · Mind Blast · Smite · **below 20 % mana: Smite rank 2 only** (keeps long fights from running dry;
without it a 5-minute fight lost 26 %). Penance as damage lost 1–3 %, so it stays a heal.

Shadow: Shadowform · SW:P if not up · Devouring Plague · Inner Focus + Mind Blast · Mind Blast ·
Shadow Word: Death · Mind Flay.

## Levelling: spells or wand?

Mana matters more than DPS while levelling, because every point of mana spent costs drinking time later.
Effective DPS = damage / (fight time + drinking time to win the mana back). MythicSim's level fixtures,
with wand damage added for the time not spent casting (the engine only fires wands for hunters):

| Effective DPS | L22 30 s / 90 s | L30 Holy 30 s / 90 s | L30 Shadow 30 s / 90 s |
|---|---|---|---|
| Smite list (spells, then drink) | 20.1 / 21.3 | **76.0 / 82.5** | **78.4 / 87.4** |
| Wand list (SW:P, Mind Blast, wand) | **25.4 / 25.6** | 62.8 / 63.7 | 75.8 / 80.2 |

So below level 25 with a wand the Wand list levels about 25 % faster; from level 30 spells and drinking
win. Between 25 and 30 there is no fixture yet: try both with `/toarot mode`.

## Talents

The addon reads your talents out of combat (classic talent API, or the trait tree if Forever uses one;
`/toarot talents` shows which) and adjusts what it tracks:

| Talent | Effect in the addon |
|---|---|
| Divine Fury | Smite and Holy Fire cast up to 0.5 s faster (queue timing) |
| Improved Mind Blast | Mind Blast cooldown up to 2.5 s shorter |
| Improved Shadow Word: Pain | SW:P lasts up to 6 s longer |
| Mental Agility | instant spells cost up to 10 % less (mana estimate) |
| Wand Specialization 2/2 | wand rotation up to level 30 instead of 25 (unless you have Searing Light) |

## Simulate your own character

1. In game, out of combat: `/toarot export`, then `/reload`.
2. On the computer: `python3 tools/sim_character.py`

It reads the export from `WTF/Account/*/SavedVariables/TOARotationHelper.lua`, builds a MythicSim request
with your level, race, stats, gear and talents, runs the Smite, Shadow and Wand rotations and prints which
levels you fastest. Needs the MythicSim engine built in `~/tools/mythicsim-forever-engine`.

## Commands

| Command | What it does |
|---|---|
| `/toarot` | Open the settings (Esc > Options > AddOns) |
| `/toarot show` / `hide` | Show or hide the display |
| `/toarot lock` | Lock or unlock (unlocked: drag with the left mouse button) |
| `/toarot reset` | Move the display back to the centre |
| `/toarot mode auto\|smite\|shadow\|wand` | Choose the rotation |
| `/toarot status` | What the engine sees: rotation, next spells, mana, tracked casts |
| `/toarot talents` | The talents the addon reads, and the wand level they give |
| `/toarot export` | Save the character for `tools/sim_character.py` (then `/reload`) |

## Limits

- Haste is not modelled: cast times come from the data. Resisted DoTs still count as applied.
- Mana regen in combat is not counted, so the mana-saving switch comes a little early (safe).
- Switching back to an earlier target only keeps its DoTs if the client lets the addon read target
  identity in combat; otherwise DoT tracking starts over on each target change.
- Keybinds are read from Blizzard's own action bars only. For the low Smite rank, put Smite rank 2 on a bar.

## Test checklist (in game)

1. `/toarot status` out of combat: *Engine: own, rotation: smite* and a spell under *Next*.
2. Hit a training dummy: the main icon follows the list above; the two icons to the right show what comes next.
3. `/toarot status` during or right after the fight: *Tracked casts* lists your casts. If it is empty,
   Forever hides your own cast events too, and the engine cannot follow you — tell the developer.
